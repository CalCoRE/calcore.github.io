Our latest paper from the [Writing Data Stories](https://corelab.berkeley.edu/blog/wds/) project has just been published in _Cognition and Instruction_. In this paper, we challenge static notions of data "relevance," instead exploring ways to help students build relationships to datasets that they may not initially perceive as related to their lives or interests.

Check out the open-access paper [here](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/07370008.2026.2727345)!
