We are pleased to announce our latest publication, which explores strategies for making large datasets manageable and pedagogically generative for K-12 educators. _How to be “Choosy”: Wrangling big datasets for the classroom_ provides a systematic approach for curriculum developers to downsample and structure public datasets in ways that support key statistical and scientific learning goals. Check out the paper [here](https://doi.org/10.1111/test.70022).
