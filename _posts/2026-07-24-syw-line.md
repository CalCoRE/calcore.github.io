This module focuses on longitudinal data and time series analysis, showing educators how to plot chronological data and measure change over time. It provides code examples for analyzing historical sensor or environmental data.

**Intended Audience & Use Case:** 
Intended for science, history, and social studies curriculum developers and teachers. It is used to design inquiry-based lessons that track and analyze change over time (such as climate change or economic shifts).
