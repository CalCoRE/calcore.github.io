Writing Data Stories Exploration Units are comprehensive, 2-3 week long curriculum units designed to build deep data literacy and statistical inquiry skills in middle school classrooms. Centered around authentic socioscientific datasets, these units provide bilingual (English/Spanish) student handouts, slides, and teacher lesson guides.

**Intended Audience & Use Case:** 
Designed for science educators, as well as curriculum developers. They provide standard-aligned (NGSS and GAISE II) unit frameworks to help students construct computational data arguments about real-world social and ecological challenges.
