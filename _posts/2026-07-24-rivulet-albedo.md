This Jupyter notebook connects to the NASA POWER API to fetch surface albedo levels anywhere on the globe. It automates the extraction of these data and allows for easy comparison of different sites.

**Intended Audience & Use Case:** 
Tailored for biology, chemistry, and environmental science educators and curriculum designers. The notebook simplifies the retrieval and comparison of surface albedo readings over time and space, allowing teachers to construct lessons comparing albedo and examining the effects of environmental events.
